
# PolicyPilot — UI/UX Direction

Build a clean, modern insurance dashboard focused on **calmness, simplicity, and trust**. Avoid generic white/black or corporate-blue insurance designs. Use **sage green, muted teal, warm cream, soft peach, and light beige** with rounded cards, subtle shadows, soft illustrations, and generous whitespace.

## 1. Login Page

* PolicyPilot logo
* Headline: **“Your insurance. Simplified.”**
* Short supporting text
* Email/phone + password
* Login
* Google/Apple login
* Calm nature/landscape illustration
* Minimal layout, no clutter

## 2. Home / Dashboard

* Sidebar:

  * Home
  * My Policies
  * Upload Policy
  * Reminders
  * Profile
* Top summary:

  * Active policies
  * Upcoming renewal
  * Total coverage
* “My Policies” cards for Health, Life and Motor
* Each card shows insurer, policy number, coverage and renewal date
* Clear **View Details** and **Upload Policy** actions

## 3. My Policies

* List/grid of all uploaded policies
* Filter by Health / Life / Motor
* Upload Policy button
* Drag-and-drop PDF/JPG/PNG upload
* After upload, show processing → successfully understood
* Policy cards should remain visually simple

## 4. Policy Details

Header:

* Policy type + icon
* Insurer
* Policy number
* Coverage
* Renewal date
* Active status
* Download Policy

Tabs:

* **Overview**
* **What's Covered**
* **Claim Process**
* **Cashless Hospitals**
* **FAQs**

Overview should show important extracted information in a simple table/card:

* Insured person
* Sum insured
* Start date
* Renewal date
* Premium
* Status

## 5. Action Cards

On the policy overview, include four simple cards:

**What's covered in your plan**
View benefits, inclusions and exclusions.

**Know your claim process**
Step-by-step claim guidance.

**Nearest cashless hospitals**
Find eligible/network hospitals.

**Frequently asked questions**
Common questions about the policy.

Use simple illustrations/icons and one clear CTA per card.

## 6. AI Chat + Voice Assistant

A **small floating PolicyPilot Assistant** should be available across all pages.

Clicking it opens a compact chat window with:

* Chat / Voice toggle
* Policy-aware conversation
* Suggested questions
* Text input
* Microphone button

Example prompts:

* “What's covered in my policy?”
* “How do I make a claim?”
* “Find cashless hospitals”
* “Explain this in simple terms”

Voice mode should show a calm listening state and allow natural conversation.

The assistant should automatically understand the **currently selected policy**, so users don't need to repeatedly specify which policy they're asking about.

## 7. UX Principles

* Calm, reassuring visual language
* Minimal buttons and navigation
* Avoid information overload
* Convert complex policy language into simple explanations
* Use progressive disclosure rather than showing everything at once
* Keep the chatbot accessible but unobtrusive
* Responsive desktop + mobile design
* Consistent design across Health, Life and Motor policies

### Core user journey

**Upload Policy → PolicyPilot understands it → View key information → Ask questions → Take action**

The product should feel like a **calm personal insurance assistant**, not a document-management system.
